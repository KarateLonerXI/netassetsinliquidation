Investment philosophy (preliminary)

Net Assets in Liquidation is an investment partnership that targets special situations and capacity-constrained opportunities.

We believe that share prices are more volatile than business values. This divergence is driven by behavioral biases that distort otherwise rational market participants and is amplified in underfollowed areas of the market characterized by illiquidity and complexity. Our objective is to exploit these inefficiencies to purchase cash flows for cheaper than their fair value, constructing a portfolio of securities worth $1 for only $0.50.

How this site handles your information

This website does not ask for, collect, or sell personal information. There are no accounts, sign-ups, comment sections, contact forms, advertisements, or analytics or tracking scripts, and nothing you type into the site is sent anywhere: the search box in the header only looks through the list of pages that is already loaded in your browser, and your search terms never leave your device. The only information the site stores is a small set of display preferences, namely your chosen text size, page width, color setting, and whether you have hidden the Contents or Appearance panels. These are saved in your browser’s local storage, not in a cookie, so that the site looks the way you left it on your next visit; they stay on your device, are never transmitted to us or to anyone else, and can be removed at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings. Like almost every website, the company that hosts these pages may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address, browser and device type, referring page, and the pages and files you request, for security, troubleshooting, and operational purposes; we do not access these logs to identify or profile visitors, and we do not combine them with any other information. This site does not embed third-party trackers, social media buttons, advertising networks, or external fonts, but some pages contain links to other websites, such as company filings, data providers, and writers I find helpful, and once you follow one of those links the other site’s own privacy policy and practices apply, over which I have no control. If you choose to email me, I will see the address and message you send and will use them only to reply; I will not add you to a mailing list or share your message. Because the site is not directed at children and does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly gather data from anyone of any age. The content here is for informational purposes only and is not investment advice. I may update this policy from time to time as the site changes, and the date at the top shows when it was last revised, so please check back if you have questions about how the site handles information.

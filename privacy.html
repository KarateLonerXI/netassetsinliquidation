Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026.

Summary

This website does not ask for, collect or sell personal information. It has no accounts, forms, advertising or analytics scripts.

What is stored in your browser

If you change the display options in the Appearance panel (text size, width, color) or hide the Contents list, your choices are saved in your browser’s local storage so they apply on your next visit. This stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can remove it by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Server logs

Like most websites, the company that hosts this site may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and operational purposes. We do not access or use these logs to identify visitors.

Third parties

This site does not embed third-party trackers. If a page links to another website, that website’s own privacy policy applies.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows when it was last changed.
